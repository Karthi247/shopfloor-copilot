# User understanding

## What supervisors need

Supervisors need to find an approved work instruction quickly while operating on a shift, often from a phone. The answer needs a traceable source, revision and page so a person can verify it against the controlled document. The assistant must be comfortable saying it cannot find an answer.

## What the product must do

- Index the supplied SOPs and maintenance/safety documents, preserving page and revision metadata.
- Retrieve only relevant passages, prioritize the current revision and retain historical versions.
- Ground generated answers in cited passages; refuse unsupported information.
- Work on desktop and mobile and show clear loading, error, source and safety states.
- Allow local deployment without publishing the service to the internet.

## Core users

- Shift supervisors looking up a procedure during production.
- Quality staff checking incoming material requirements.
- Maintenance staff consulting machine maintenance instructions.
- Safety staff consulting the lockout/tagout procedure.

## Assumptions to validate with the customer

- The supplied PDFs are approved and current source documents.
- Document number and revision are available in PDF text or can be reviewed during ingestion.
- The intended pilot is a trusted private Wi-Fi environment.
- English is sufficient for the first pilot.
- A human-controlled document change process exists outside this prototype.

## Questions for discovery

Who is authorized to approve answers? How are obsolete revisions marked? Are there confidential procedures or role-specific permissions? What should happen when the SOP is silent? Which languages and device sizes are used on the floor? What uptime and response-time targets matter? How are updates approved and audited?
